## Money

Money is the primary currency on the server. It is required to promote your star ranks, purchase industrial supplies from the server `/shop`, trade with other players, and finance town infrastructure.

### Earning Money

Generating steady revenue is necessary to fund town upkeep and expand corporate nations. You can build wealth through several methods:
* Voting for the server daily.
* Participating in active piñata parties.
* Selling automated farm outputs directly to the `/shop`.
* Trading resources with other players.
* Opening basic reward capsules.

You can verify your personal financial standing using the `/balance` command.

### Voting for the Server

Submit server votes via the [/vote](/home/vote/) command to receive **Basic Capsule keys**. Unlocking these capsules drops money, raw experience points, and rare components, offering the most straightforward method for early-game capital injection.

### Joining the Piñata Party

The community triggers a server-wide piñata event automatically for every **75 collective votes** recorded. Damaging the active piñata distributes random **Piñata Pouches**[^1] directly to participating players. When the boss is successfully cleared, every online player receives a **Piñata Capsule key** as a server-wide bonus.

### Selling Items at the Shop

The server marketplace is accessed instantly via the `/shop` command. The server shop acts as an infinite source of liquidity, allowing you to convert high-volume output from crop farms and mob grinders directly into server cash.

## Glims

Glims function as a play-to-earn engagement currency used to acquire high-tier spawners, utility keys, and premium cosmetics. You can transfer funds to other community members using the `/trade [playerName]` command.

### Earning Glims

Glims are highly scarce and track active server participation. You can secure this currency through distinct gameplay channels:
* Voting for the server on tracking lists.
* Spending time or going AFK inside the [Glim Hub](/earth/gameplay/worlds/#glim-hub).
* Trading with other players in the open market.
* Opening basic or piñata capsules.

Review your active totals using the `/glims` command.

***

## Stellar Residues

Stellar Residues are rare exchange materials used by **Silas, the Star Broker** at `/spawn`. Each trade requires **3 Stellar Residues** and an **undamaged Elytra**, rewarding your choice of a Dragon Head, Heavy Core, or Heart of the Sea.

You can obtain Stellar Residues from [Basic and Piñata Capsules](/earth/gameplay/capsules/), through certain capsule bundle rewards, or by trading **250,000 experience points** and an **undamaged Elytra** with Silas.

***

## Gems

Gems serve as the premium currency on the server, utilized to purchase exclusive cosmetics, seasonal capsules, and premium items. You can transfer gems to other players using the `/trade [playerName]` command.

### Obtaining Gems

Gems are secured primarily by supporting infrastructure costs via the [Vista Store](https://store.vistavalley.net/). You can check your available balance using the `/gems` command.

[^1]: Pouches drop a randomized selection of money, experience points, glims, capsule keys, Hearts of the Sea, Heavy Cores, Elytras, netherite scrap, netherite ingots, and vessels.
