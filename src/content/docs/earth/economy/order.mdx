---
title: Order
---

The *Order* feature lets you order items or deliver items for money. You can order a certain amount of items for players to deliver and pay money for each item in exchange or you could deliver orders to earn money for each delivery.

To open the *Order* GUI type the command /order

##Delivering A Order

To deliver a order just click the order which you want to deliver to with the requested items

##Making Orders

To make a order, go to *Your Orders* and then *New Order*
