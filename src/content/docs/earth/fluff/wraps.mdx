**Wraps** are unique cosmetic skins that allow you to customize the appearance of your gear including swords, armor, elytras, and more without changing their stats or enchantments.

## How to Obtain Wraps

Wraps cannot be crafted. They are exclusive cosmetic drops obtained through opening capsules:

* **Seasonal Capsules:** Available during special events and seasonal server updates.
* **Elytra Emporium Capsules:** Specialized capsules containing elytra cosmetics.

## How to Apply a Wrap

Applying a wrap to your gear is quick and easy:
<Steps>
1. **Hold the item** you want to customize in your main hand (e.g., your sword, armor piece, or elytra).
2. Type the following command in chat: `/wraps`
3. A custom menu will open showing all available wraps for that specific item type.
4. **Click the wrap** you wish to equip.
</Steps>
To remove or swap a wrap, simply hold the item, run `/wraps` again, and select a different skin or choose the default option to unequip it.

:::note[Purely Cosmetic]
Wraps are all cosmetic and do **not** enhance the performance or durabilty of your specific gears!
:::
