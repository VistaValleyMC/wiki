Custom enchantments allow you to upgrade your tools, weapons, and armor with specialized abilities that extend far beyond vanilla limits. These modifiers are essential for optimizing high-output industrial farms, accelerating resource gathering, and dominating PvP/PvE encounters.

### How to Obtain

Most custom enchantments are purchased using experience points via the `/archmage` command. Your purchase awards an enchantment orb corresponding to your selected tier.

:::important[Unlock Requirement]
Access to the `/archmage` marketplace requires players to reach [**11 🌟**](/earth/gameplay/stars/#star-11).
:::

Right-clicking an enchantment orb rolls a random custom enchantment book from that specific tier, displaying its success and break rates.

### How to Apply

Applying custom enchantments carries an inherent risk. If the application process fails, the enchantment book will be lost, and it may completely **destroy your equipment** unless it is properly protected using item modifications.

:::important[Slot Limits]
By default, all tools and armor pieces are limited to a maximum of **5 custom enchantment slots**.
:::

To enchant an item, open your inventory, click to select the custom enchantment book, and click directly on top of the equipment piece you wish to upgrade.

Unless an enchantment lists a narrower scope, weapon-based combat effects treat swords, axes, bows, crossbows, tridents, maces, and spears as weapons. Maces and spears also trigger eligible opponent enchantments during combat.

These utility items protect your high-tier gear investments and optimize enchantment success rates. Many of these components must be purchased from **Althea** inside the **Glim Hub** via `/glimhub`.

### Fortification Scroll

This scroll acts as insurance during the enchanting process. If a custom enchantment attempt fails, the Fortification Scroll is consumed, but your equipment remains completely intact and protected from destruction.
* Costs **2,500 Glims** at Althea's shop.
* Apply by picking up the scroll in your inventory and clicking it directly onto your equipment.

### Arcane Dust

This material increases the raw success rate of an enchantment book by a value of **1% to 5%** depending on its tier. Opening a container of **Sealed Dust** can occasionally fail, resulting in **Mystery Dust** which contains alternative functions.
* Acquired through the `/tinkerer` interface by trading away unwanted custom enchantment books.
* Accessing the `/tinkerer` marketplace requires players to reach [**Star 15 🌟**](/earth/gameplay/stars/#star-15).
* To use, right-click Sealed Dust to reveal its percentage value, then select the dust and click it onto your desired enchantment book.

### Custom Enchantment Randomiser

This tool completely rerolls the success and break percentages of a custom enchantment book. This is useful for saving books with exceptionally low baseline success rates before investing expensive Arcane Dust. The results are randomized and can lower the stats further.
* Purchased from Althea at a price ranging from **50,000 to 625,000 EXP**, scaling with the book's tier.
* Apply by selecting the randomizer in your inventory and clicking it directly onto the target book.

### Extraction Scroll

This utility isolates a single random custom enchantment from an active piece of equipment, safely stripping it off and turning it back into an applicable book with randomized success stats.
* Costs **1,250 Glims** at Althea's shop.
* Apply by picking up the scroll in your inventory and clicking it directly onto the enchanted equipment.

### Slot Expansion Gems

This item overrides default gear limitations by increasing the total custom enchantment slots available on a single piece of equipment up to a maximum threshold of **5 slots**.
* Costs **25,000 Glims** at Althea's shop.
* Apply by picking up the gem in your inventory and clicking it directly onto your equipment.

### Stack Culler

This active service applies a temporary personal status buff that permits players to instantly clear out entire stacks of stacked mobs for a duration of 30 minutes. The timer activates instantly upon transaction.
* Costs **1,250 Glims** at Althea's shop.
* Available as a permanent milestone reward across various late-game [Star Ranks](/earth/gameplay/stars).
