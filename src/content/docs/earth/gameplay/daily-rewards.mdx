title: Daily Rewards
description: Claim your daily bonuses, engagement currencies, and pass rewards.
---

Access the daily rewards menu in-game using the `/daily` or `/dailyrewards` commands. You can claim any rewards listed below provided you meet their specific prerequisites.

## Resident Reward

Players can claim one 🔮 [**Mystic Capsule**](/earth/gameplay/capsules/#mystic-capsule) key every day if the following requirements are met:
1. Reach [Star 5 🌟](/earth/gameplay/stars/#star-5) or higher.
2. Link your account to our Discord server.

## Glim Collector Pass

This 30-day subscription focuses on accumulating play-to-earn currency. You can purchase this pass on the [Vista Store](https://store.vistavalley.net).

* Receive an upfront bonus of **8,000 glims** instantly upon purchase.
* Claim **1,400 glims** every day for 30 days, yielding a total value of **50,000 glims**.
* Missed daily claims do not stack; you must log in and play for at least 30 minutes daily to collect your currency.

## Gem Pioneer Pass

This 30-day subscription provides a steady stream of premium currency. You can purchase this pass on the [Vista Store](https://store.vistavalley.net).

* Receive an upfront bonus of **300 gems** instantly upon purchase.
* Claim **40 gems** every day for 30 days, yielding a total value of **1,500 gems**.
* Missed daily claims do not stack and must be collected each calendar day.

## Booster Pass

Support the community by boosting the [Vista Valley Discord](https://discord.vistavalley.net/) server to unlock an ongoing daily payout.

* Claim **250 glims** every single day as long as your Discord boost is actively maintained.
* Missed daily claims do not stack and cannot be recovered.

## Culler Pass

This 30-day subscription provides industrial automation utility for your farms. You can purchase this pass on the [Vista Store](https://store.vistavalley.net).

* Claim **45 minutes of Stack Culler** utility every day for 30 days to optimize your grinding workflow.
* Missed daily claims do not stack and must be collected each day.

***

:::note
All daily rewards reset every day at **12 AM server time**.
:::
