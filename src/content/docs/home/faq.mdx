In this page, we answer some of the most frequently asked questions about Vista Valley.

## What can I do on the server?

Vista Valley is an economy-driven server with activities for every playstyle:

- **Build & Develop:** Create or join a town, claim land, and design your base.
- **Economy & Trade:** Run shops, sell items in `/shop`, trade with players, and manage town or nation finances.
- **Farming & Automation:** Build farms and grinders (cactus, crops, mob farms) to generate steady income.
- **PvP & Events:** Take part in player-run PvP arenas and seasonal events.
- **Custom Enchantments & Gear:** Discover and obtain unique custom enchantments via capsules, the shops, or trade with players; apply them to tools, weapons, and armor to grant special abilities beyond vanilla enchantments.
- **Warps & Tourism:** Set up warps to attract visitors and grow your business.
- **Social & Community:** Join community projects, use voice chat, or hang out.

## How many currencies are there on the server?

There are three currencies on Vista Valley: **Money**, **Glims**, and **Gems**. Money is the primary currency used for ranking up and general trading. Glims are a secondary, player-driven currency mainly spent in the `/glimshop`. Gems are the premium currency and are typically obtained by purchasing them from the store.

## What is money used for?

[Money](/earth/economy/#money) is the primary currency on Vista Valley. It is used to rank up your town and nation, purchase items from `/shop`, and trade with other players.

## How do I make money?

[Money](/earth/economy/#money) can be earned through capsule rewards, selling items in `/shop`, or trading with other players.

## What is the best way to make money?

Building farms that produce large quantities of sellable items is the most effective long-term strategy. A classic example is a cactus farm, which generates a steady income through `/shop` sales.

## What is an easy way to make money?

A beginner-friendly method is to go AFK in Glim Hub to accumulate Glims, then sell them to other players for money.

## What are Glims?

[Glims](/earth/economy/#glims) are the freemium currency earned through various in-game activities. They are primarily spent in the `/glimshop` or Althea.

## How do I earn Glims?

You can earn Glims in several ways, but the primary method is hanging out (or AFK) in Glim Hub. You can also earn Glims by voting, owning a Glims Pass, opening capsules, or purchasing them from other players.

## What are Gems?

[Gems](/earth/economy/#gems) are the premium currency. They are mainly purchased from the store and are used to buy premium cosmetics, capsules, and exclusive items.

## Will I lose items upon death?

No, you will not! In this server, keep inventory and experience are enabled, however death does come at a cost! Some money will be taken away from your personal balance, town, and nation bank.

## Is there a way to get enchantments faster?

Yes. Visit villager hubs at certain warps to trade for vanilla enchanted books. Custom enchants come from `/archmage`, capsules, or trade with players. See [custom enchantments](/earth/gameplay/custom-enchantments/).

## How do I use Warps?

You can use the [Warps](/earth/gameplay/warps/) feature to quickly teleport to various locations around the server. Check out the relevant page for more details.

## Is Voice Chat mod supported?

Yes! Vista Valley supports the [Simple Voice Chat](/earth/social/simple-voice-chat/) mod for in-game voice communication.

## What are Wraps?

[Wraps](/earth/fluff/wraps/) are decorative items that you can use to customize the appearance of your tools and weapons. Visit the Wraps page to learn more about how to obtain and use them.
