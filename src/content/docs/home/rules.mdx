## Server Rules

If You Have Any Questions About These Rules, Please Ask A Staff Member On Our [Discord Server](https://discord.vistavalley.net) Before Taking Any Action That May Violate Them.

:::caution[Account Responsibility & Security]
You Are Entirely Responsible For All Actions Taken On Your Account. Vista Valley Does Not Ban Players Solely For Allowing A Trusted Friend To Maintain A Daily Login Streak, But Sharing Account Credentials Is Strictly Unsupported And Undertaken At Your Own Risk.

Staff Will Not Investigate, Rollback, Or Recover Items, Money, Or Land Lost Due To Shared Passwords Or Account Borrowing. Furthermore, Your Account Inherits The Full Network Reputation And Moderation History Of Anyone Who Logs Into It. If Your Account Connects From An IP Address Associated With An Active Ban, Cheating Offense, Or Severe Infraction, Your Account Will Be Penalized Under Our Ban Evasion Policies Regardless Of Who Was At The Keyboard. Keep Your Credentials Secure.
:::

:::caution[Group And Town Responsibility]
Vista Valley Does Not Tolerate Coordinated Toxicity. If Members Of A Town, Nation, Or Alliance Are Found To Be Directly Coordinating, Encouraging, Or Repeatedly Participating Together In Severe Rule Violations (Such As Hate Speech, Slurs, Or Targeted Harassment), The Entire Group Or Town May Face Collective Punishment. This Includes Permanent Bans For All Associated Accounts And The Complete Deletion Of Town Builds Or Assets. Choose Your Town Members And Associates Carefully; Their Actions Can Sink Your Entire Group.
:::

### Use Common Sense

This Is The Foundational Rule. Even If Something Is Not Explicitly Listed, It May Still Be Enforceable. Staff Have The Final Say, And Severe Violations May Skip Warning Steps. If Unsure About A Rule Or Mechanic, Open A Ticket And Ask Before Proceeding. While Honest Misunderstandings Regarding Ambiguous Guidelines Will Be Handled Through Education Or Rollbacks Where Possible, Deliberate Exploitation Of Game Systems Or Economic Progression Will Result In Immediate Penalties Regardless Of Claimed Ignorance.

### Chat Etiquette & Respectful Conduct

Good Faith And Light-Hearted Banter Are Welcome, But Cease Immediately If Someone Expresses Discomfort, And Apologize. This Server Is For Relaxation And Enjoyment; Disrespectful Behavior Disrupts Everyone's Experience And Fosters Toxicity. Remember, There's A Person On The Other Side Of The Screen.

Prohibited Behavior Includes, But Is Not Limited To:

* Hate Speech Or Language That Attacks Or Demeans A Person Or Group Based On Race, Ethnicity, National Origin, Religion, Sexual Orientation, Gender, Gender Identity, Disability, Or Other Protected Characteristics.
* Targeted Insults, Belittling Language, Or Personal Attacks Intended To Demean Another User.
* Harassment, Including Repeated Unwelcome Contact, Stalking, Or Creating A Hostile Environment.
* Language Suggesting Or Implying Physical Harm Or Violence Toward Others.
* Suicide Jokes Or Encouragement Of Self-Destructive Behavior.
* Language Intended To Demean Or Marginalize Political Or Religious Beliefs.
* Racial Slurs And Derogatory Terms, Regardless Of Context, Jokes, Or Self-Referential Use.
* Glorification Or Excessive Referencing Of Historical Or Contemporary Ideologies That Promote Hatred, Discrimination, Or Violence.
* Holding Extended Conversations In Languages Other Than English, Bahasa Melayu, Or Bahasa Indonesia In Public Chat Channels. Brief Greetings, Harmless Expressions, Or Casual Code-Switching In Other Languages Are Fine, Provided The Main Conversation Remains Understandable To Staff (Other Languages Are Fully Permitted In Town, Local, And/Or Similarly Private Chat Channels).
* Excessively Pinging Offline Players For Non-Urgent Matters, Mentioning Users Who Asked Not To Be Pinged, Or Mass-Pinging Roles For General Questions.
* Repeatedly Sending Unwanted Private Messages After Being Asked To Stop.
* Intentionally Flooding Public Or Private Channels With Meaningless Messages.
* Using Signs, Books, Item Names, Or Other In-Game Features To Distribute Hateful Or Harassing Messages.

While Swearing (Basic Profanity) Is Allowed In Moderation, It Must Never Cross Into Targeted Harassment Or The Use Of Restricted Terms. Under No Circumstances Are Hate Speech, Racial Slurs, Or Highly Derogatory Terms Permitted Anywhere On The Server. This Restriction Is Absolute, As It Applies Even If The Words Are Used Casually, In Private, As A Joke, Or With The Full Consent Of Everyone Involved In The Conversation.

### No NSFW Or Explicit Content

This Minecraft Server Is Accessible To Minors. Therefore, Any Content That Is Sexually Suggestive, Graphically Violent, Or Otherwise Intended For Mature Audiences Is Strictly Prohibited. This Includes, But Is Not Limited To:

* Explicit Textual Descriptions.
* Sexualized Images Or Videos.
* Graphic Depictions Of Violence Or Gore.
* Links To External NSFW Websites.

While Private Chat Channels May Have More Relaxed Conversations, Any Content That Violates This Rule And Is Reported Will Be Treated As A Global Incident And Subject To Moderation. The Safety And Comfort Of All Users, Especially Minors, Is Our Priority.

:::note[Private Messaging Policy]
Just Like Public Chat, Private Messaging Channels (Such As Local Chat, Town Chat, And Such) Are Subject To Server Rules. While Staff May Not Be Actively Monitoring Private Logs At Times, Any Hate Speech, Slurs, Or Targeted Harassment Reported Via A Support Ticket Will Be Treated As A Global Incident And Moderated With Standard Server Penalties.
:::

### No Cheating, Hacking, Or Exploiting

Any Attempt To Gain An Unfair Advantage Through External Software, Unauthorized Client Modifications, Diagnostic Tools, Or Game Engine Exploits Is Strictly Prohibited. Vista Valley Is Intended To Provide A Balanced Survival Experience, And Circumventing Intended Mechanics Damages That Balance.

The Server's Anticheat May Automatically Flag Or Kick Players Suspected Of Cheating, In Addition To Manual Staff Review.

Please Refer To The Dropdown Menus Below For Detailed Specifications On Modifications And Mechanics.

<details>

<summary>Prohibited Client Modifications</summary>

The Following Third-Party Software, Client Modifications, And Asset Packages Are Strictly Prohibited:

* Unverified Modifications, Cheat Clients, Injectors, Or Hacks Not Explicitly Approved By Server Staff.
* PvP Advantage Modifications, Including Aimbots, Killauras, Reach Modifiers, Speed Hacks, And Fly Hacks.
* X-Ray Modifications, Transparent Texture Packs, Or Cave-Finder Shaders That Reveal Hidden Blocks And Resources.
* Minimaps Or World Maps Featuring Cave Mode, Underground Radar, Or Player/Mob Tracking.
* Automation Software, External Macros, Or Hardware Tools Used For Unattended Actions.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Prohibited Game Exploits & Engine Abuse</summary>

Using Vanilla Mechanics, Client Diagnostic Screens, Or Game Engine Oversights To Bypass Intended Survival Progression Is Strictly Prohibited, Regardless Of Whether You Are Playing On An Unmodded Client:

* Profiler Navigation And Debug Abuse (Such As Using The Pie Chart Or Render Metrics) To Locate Hidden Spawners, Chests, Subterranean Structures, Or Ores Through Solid Ground.
* Block Phasing, Composter/Piston Camera Suffocation Tricks, Or Boat Glitches Used To View Through Solid Terrain.
* Item Or Block Duplication Glitches Outside Intended In-Game Mechanics.
* Nether Roof Exploits Beyond What Is Explicitly Permitted By Server Staff.
* Glitches Designed To Crash Chunks, Induce Server Lag, Or Manipulate Tick Rates.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Allowed Modifications</summary>

The Following Modifications Are Permitted For Use On The Server:

* Minimaps And Waypoint Mods (Surface Terrain Only, Cave Mode And Entity Tracking Disabled).
* Recipe Viewer Mods Such As JustEnoughItems (JEI) Or NotEnoughItems (NEI).
* Performance Optimization Mods Such As OptiFine, Sodium, Or Iris.
* Chunk Caching Mods Such As Bobby.
* Fullbright Mods For Improved Visibility In Dark Areas.
* Schematica Or Litematica (Hologram Display Only; Printer/Automated Placement Is Prohibited).
* Cosmetic Resource Packs And Shaders.
* Personal Stat Displays, Inventory Sorters, And Shulker Box Peekers.

</details>

:::note[Additional Guidelines]
1. If You Are Unsure Of The Legality Of Any Third-Party Modification, Resource Pack, Or Shader, Ask Staff Before Enabling It.
2. Any External Client Modification Not Listed Under Allowed Modifications Should Be Assumed Banned Until Reviewed By Staff.
3. Claiming That An Exploit Relies On Vanilla Minecraft, Built-In Debug Menus, Or Unmodded Behavior Does Not Exempt It From Exploit Rules.
4. If You Would Like To Propose An Amendment Or Request Approval For A Specific Utility Mod, Open A Ticket On Our [Discord Server](https://discord.vistavalley.net). You Will Not Be Punished For Asking.
:::

### No Griefing Or Stealing

Stealing And Griefing Are Universally Prohibited Across All Protected Areas And Town Claims. This Policy Applies Strictly Regardless Of Town Residency: Joining A Town, Being Added As A Resident, Or Holding Plot Permissions Does Not Grant Permission To Empty Town Vaults, Raid Community Chests, Or Alter Builds Without Explicit Permission From Town Leadership. Town Insiding Is Treated Directly As Theft And Griefing.

In Unclaimed Wilderness, Basic Raiding Of Abandoned Builds And Looting Exposed Containers Is Permitted, Provided The Surrounding Landscape Remains Undamaged. Destroying Terrain, Creating Landscape Scars, Lava Casting, Or Flooding During A Raid Violates The [Earth Preservation Program](#earth-preservation-program). Intentional Destruction Near Town Borders Or Creating Claims To Encircle And Restrict A Town's Growth Is Strictly Prohibited.

:::note[Note On Shared Spaces]
Public Grinding Zones, Shared Warps, And Open-World Resource Areas Are Accessible To All Players. Competing For Mob Kills Or Loose Experience Orbs In These Areas Is Considered Regular Gameplay And Is Not A Moderatable Offense. However, If A Player Repeatedly Tracks, Blocks, Or Follows You Across Multiple Locations Specifically To Prevent You From Playing, It May Be Reviewed Under Our Harassment Policy.
:::

### No Unsolicited Advertising

We Strictly Prohibit Unsolicited Advertisements, Including Promoting External Servers, Websites, Or Services. Please Refrain From Any Form Of Spam Or Self-Promotion Outside Of Designated Channels Or Areas Within Our Community. Responsible Sharing Of Information Within Our Community Is Welcome.

### Multiple Accounts, Household Connections, & Ban Evasion

Each Player Is Permitted Only One Primary Java Account. Having Multiple Active Accounts Owned Or Controlled By The Same Person Is Strictly Prohibited, With The Exception Of The Single Permitted Bedrock Cross-Play Account Outlined Below. The Use Of Bot Accounts For Any Purpose Is Strictly Forbidden.

Separate Family Members, Roommates, Or Housemates Playing On The Same Local Network (Sharing An IP Address) Are Fully Welcome, Provided Each Account Belongs To And Is Played By A Unique Individual. To Prevent Automated Flags Or Misunderstandings During Moderation Reviews, Shared Households Should Open A Brief Verification Ticket On Our Discord Server To Inform Staff.

Attempting To Bypass Or Evade Any Active Moderation Penalty (Such As Bans, Mutes, Or Temporary Suspensions) Using Alternate Accounts, VPNs, Proxies, Or Shared Household Accounts Is Strictly Prohibited. Any Alternate Account Used For Ban Evasion Will Be Permanently Banned Immediately, And The Penalty On Your Primary Account Will Be Significantly Extended Or Upgraded To A Permanent Ban.

#### Cross-Platform Accounts (Java And Bedrock)

* Java Edition Players Are Permitted To Register One (1) Alternate Bedrock Edition Account For Cross-Play Functionality.
* Information On Joining The Server Via Bedrock Edition Can Be Found [Here](https://wiki.vistavalley.net/play-now#bedrock-edition).
* Your Secondary Bedrock Account Must Not Be Online While Your Primary Java Account Is Active, Except For Limited Circumstances Such As Town Invites Or Pre-Notified Item Swaps.
* Your Secondary Bedrock Account Must Reside Within The Same Town (Or Nation) As Your Primary Java Account.
* Your Secondary Bedrock Account Cannot Manage Its Own Town.

### No Scamming

Scamming Other Players Is Strictly Prohibited. All Agreements Must Be Upheld Honestly And Completely. This Includes, But Is Not Limited To:

* Deliberate Deception Or Omission Of Details During Trades.
* Selling Renamed Items To Mislead Others About Their Value.
* Selling Mundane Items As Valuable Or Rare Items (Such As Selling A Candle As A "Capsule Key" For $5,000).

Obvious Joke Items, Like "Holy Water" (A Renamed Bottle Of Water Sold For $50), Are Permitted, Provided They Are Clearly Presented As A Joke.

Valid Proof Of Agreements Is Required, Such As In-Game Chat Screenshots Or Signed Books. Discord Screenshots Can Be Used As Supplementary Evidence But Are Not Considered Primary Proof Due To Verification Limitations.

### No Sharing Personal Information

Sharing Another Person's Personal Information Without Their Explicit Consent Is Strictly Prohibited. This Includes, But Is Not Limited To, Addresses, Passwords, And Any Other Sensitive Data.

### No Real-Money Trading (IRL Trading)

Trading In-Game Items, Currency, Towns, Claims, Or Services For Real-World Money, Gift Cards, Or External Goods And Services Is Strictly Prohibited. The Server Economy Relies On Player Effort And Fair Progression, And External Financial Deals Disrupt This Balance.

This Includes, But Is Not Limited To:

* Exchanging Real-World Currency, Gift Cards, Or Crypto For In-Game Currency, Items, Towns, Or Claims.
* Paying Real Money For Building, Mining, Or In-Game Labor (Or Vice Versa).
* Exchanging Vista Valley Assets For Items, Currency, Or Accounts On Other Platforms Or Games.

Any Attempts To Facilitate, Advertise, Or Execute Real-Money Transactions Will Result In Severe Punishments, Up To A Permanent Ban. The Staff Team Will Not Investigate, Mediate, Or Recover Assets Lost To External Deals Gone Wrong. If You Are Approached By Someone Offering Real Money For Your In-Game Assets, Please Report It Via A Ticket On Our [Discord Server](https://discord.vistavalley.net).

### Webstore Purchases & Chargeback Policy

All Purchases Made On The Official Vista Valley Webstore Are Voluntary And Processed Through PayNow. By Purchasing Virtual Items, Ranks, Subscriptions, Or Perks, You Agree That All Transactions Are Final And Non-Refundable.

Guidelines Regarding Store Purchases Include:

* Subscriptions Can Be Canceled At Any Time Via The PayNow Portal (`https://checkout.paynow.gg/subscriptions`). Cancellation Stops Future Recurring Payments But Does Not Entitle You To A Refund For Past Payments Or Prorated Billing Cycles.
* Initiating A Chargeback, Payment Dispute, Or Unauthorized Reversal Through Your Bank, Credit Card Issuer, Or Payment Provider Without Prior Approval From Staff Will Result In An Immediate, Permanent Ban Across All Associated Accounts And IPs.
* If You Encounter An Issue With A Transaction (Such As Missing Items Or Checkout Errors), Please Open A Ticket On Our [Discord Server](https://discord.vistavalley.net) So Staff Can Assist You Before Taking Any External Financial Action.

### Respectful Combat

Player-Versus-Player (PvP) Combat Is Prohibited Unless Explicitly Agreed Upon By All Involved Parties Prior To Engagement. Logging Out During Combat To Evade Conflict Is Also Prohibited.

#### Permitted Combat Scenarios

* Agreed-Upon Duels Mutually Consented To By All Participants.
* Town Outlaw Combat Initiated Against Players Designated As Outlaws Within A Town's Jurisdiction.
* Declared Wars Occurring Within The Context Of A Formally Declared War Between Towns Or Nations.

## Server Guidelines

### Earth Preservation Program

We Have Implemented Many Rules And Systems To Increase The Longevity Of The Earth Map, Ensuring We Do Not Have To Reset It While Keeping It Fun To Play On.

To Maintain The Earth's Environment, We Also Limit Certain Activities Above Ground. We Do Not Intend To Reset The Map, So Ensuring It's Well Kept Is Pivotal For A Positive Experience For All. Activities That Are Prohibited Include, But Are Not Limited To:

* Creating Forest Fires.
* Flooding Areas.
* Lava Casting.
* Large-Scale Quarrying (Within Reason; This Rule Does Not Apply Within Your Town's Borders).

We Also Regulate Some Towny Aspects To Keep Earth Fair And Habitable For All. This Includes Banning Snake-Claims And Misuse Of Outposts.

* Snake Claims Are Long, Thin Claims That Usually Connect Two Faraway Claims Or Outline An Area. Outlining With Claims Is Allowed Sometimes Under The Assumption It Will Be Filled Out Within 14 Days. Claims That Are Not Substantially Developed Or Filled In Within 14 Days Of Creation May Be Considered Obstructive And Subject To Staff Removal Following A Warning.
* Any Land A Town Owns That Is Not Connected To The Main Spawn Must Include An Outpost Plot. The Exception Is If There Is A Close-Knit Island Chain Or Archipelago, Where Only One Outpost Plot Or Home Block Is Needed. Contact Staff If You Are Unsure About Outpost Legality.

#### Fallen Towns, Ruin Looting, & Build Protection

When A Town Falls Into Ruin Due To Mayor/Resident Inactivity Or Failure To Pay Towny Upkeep, All Protections On Its Contents Are Permanently Voided. Staff Will **Not** Issue Rollbacks, Restore Lost Items, Or Punish Raiders If Town Members Return After Their Town Has Fallen—Even If They Log Back In The Very Next Day. Maintaining Upkeep And Activity Is The Sole Responsibility Of The Town.

However, The Longevity Of Vista Valley Relies On Preserving Community History And Landmarks On The Earth Map. When Scavenging A Fallen Town, Players Must Follow Strict Boundaries Between **Looting Valuables** And **Destroying Architecture**:

- **Permitted Looting (Free-for-All):** Players May Freely Loot All Containers (Chests, Barrels, Shulker Boxes), Extract Items From Frames And Armor Stands, And Mine High-Value Functional Blocks Such As Mob Spawners, Beacons (Including Their Mineral Pyramid Bases), Anvils, Enchanting Tables, And Redstone/Sorting Components. Breaking Minor Doorways To Access Sealed Rooms Is Permitted.
- **Prohibited Destruction (Build Protection):** Intentionally Destroying, Flattening, Stripping, Or Griefing The Physical Architecture Of Long-Standing Builds, Monuments, Or Recognizable Landmarks Is Strictly Prohibited. You May Not Strip Decorative Walls, Roofs, Or Floors For Building Materials.

Leave The Structures Intact For Future Explorers Or New Towns To Repurpose. Players Who Obliterate Historic Builds Or Vandalize Landmark Architecture During A Raid Will Face Severe Penalties, And Structural Damage Will Be Rolled Back.

### Redstone, Automation, & AFK Grinding

We Maintain Specific Guidelines Regarding Automation And Redstone Machines To Balance Performance, Quality Of Life, And Fair Gameplay.

:::caution[Automation & AFK Rules]
Automating Player Actions (Such As Attacking, Placing Blocks, Or Fishing) While Away From Your Keyboard Is Strictly Forbidden. This Includes The Use Of Auto-Clickers, Scripts, Macros, Or Taping Down A Mouse Button. However, Passive AFK Is Permitted For Activities That Do Not Require Automated Input, Such As Standing In The [Glim Hub](/earth/gameplay/worlds/#glim-hub) For Passive Income Or Keeping Chunks Loaded For A Redstone Farm.

To Reduce Tedious Grinding, You Are Permitted To Use Tools Like An Auto-Clicker For Specific Tasks *Only* While You Are Present At Your Computer And Actively Attentive. Permitted Attended Tasks Include Repetitive Non-Combat Work (Like Breaking Blocks In A Generator Or Clearing Terrain) And Attacking Mobs In A Farm Or Grinder. If Staff Conduct An In-Game Verification Check, You Will Be Given **1 To 2 Minutes** To Respond. Failure To Respond Within This Window Will Be Treated As Unattended Automation.
:::

<details>

<summary>Prohibited Machines And Farms</summary>

* Machines Built With The Intention Of Causing Server Lag.
* Machines Made To Bypass Vanilla Game Mechanics, Such As Bedrock Breakers.
* 0-Tick Farms Or Duplication Mechanisms.
* Fast Pulsing Redstone Clocks, Including Doubled Observers Acting As Fast Clocks.
* Farms Utilizing Excessive Entity Cramming.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Allowed Machines And Farms</summary>

* Semi-Automatic Farms Requiring Player Interaction (E.g., Standard Cactus Or Sugarcane Setups Where Players Initiate Planting).
* Fully Automatic Farms For Crops That Regrow Without Replanting (E.g., Pumpkins, Melons, Bamboo).
* Mob Grinders Utilizing Minimal Redstone.
* Automatic Smelting And Item Sorting Systems.
* Non-Redstone Automated Setups, Such As Iron Golem Farms And Villager Breeders.
* Flying Machines With A Set Endpoint, Switch Activation, And Non-Continuous Movement.

</details>

:::danger[Laggy Towns]
If Staff Detect Significant Lag Caused By A Town, They Will Issue A Warning To The Owner(s), Requesting Adjustments Within **24 Hours**. Failure To Comply May Lead To Staff Intervention To Preserve Server Performance. Proper Management Of Farms, Animal Pens, Redstone Contraptions, And Similar Setups Helps Maintain A Smoother Experience For Everyone.
:::

### Player Shops & Market Conduct

Player-Run Shops Are An Important Part Of The Vista Valley Economy. Players Are Encouraged To Establish Shops, Negotiate Prices, And Participate In Fair Trading.

The Following Activities Are Prohibited:

* Deliberately Creating Misleading Shop Advertisements Or Falsely Describing Products.
* Advertising An Item As Enchanted, Rare, Or Of A Particular Quality When It Does Not Possess Those Properties.
* Using Shop Systems Or Transaction Mechanics To Duplicate Currency Or Items.
* Manipulating Shop Interfaces Or Payment Systems Through Unintended Mechanics.
* Intentionally Exploiting Another Player's Transaction Mistake Through A Known Shop Bug.
* Creating Shops Or Advertisements That Impersonate Official Server Stores.
* Using Alternate Accounts To Manipulate Shop Transactions, Rankings, Or Economic Statistics.

:::note[Shop Transactions]
Always Verify The Item, Quantity, And Price Before Completing A Purchase. Players Are Responsible For Checking Ordinary Transaction Details. Report Suspected Shop-System Bugs Or Duplicated Transactions To Staff Immediately.
:::

### Resource World Guidelines

Resource Worlds Exist To Provide Players With Opportunities To Gather Materials Without Unnecessarily Damaging The Permanent Earth Map.

Players Must Follow These Guidelines:

* Do Not Exploit Resource-World Regeneration Or Reset Mechanics To Duplicate Resources.
* Do Not Use Prohibited Modifications To Locate Hidden Ores, Structures, Or Containers.
* Do Not Intentionally Create Lag Machines Or Excessive Entity Collections In Resource Worlds.
* Do Not Exploit World Transitions To Bypass Inventory Or Gameplay Restrictions.
* Do Not Intentionally Prevent Other Players From Accessing Public Resource Areas Through Repeated Obstruction Or Harassment.
* Follow Any Additional Restrictions Announced For Individual Resource Worlds.

:::note[Resource World Reminder]
Normal Competition For Resources, Mining Locations, Mobs, And Naturally Generated Materials Is Permitted. However, Exploiting Bugs Or Deliberately Disrupting Other Players' Gameplay Is Not.
:::

### Towny Permissions & Resident Responsibilities

Town Membership And Plot Permissions Provide Access To Certain Areas And Facilities, But They Do Not Grant Unlimited Ownership Over Another Player's Belongings.

Players Should Follow These Guidelines:

* Do Not Access, Modify, Or Remove Another Resident's Private Storage Without Permission.
* Do Not Use Trusted Permissions To Damage Another Player's Property.
* Do Not Remove Or Alter Another Resident's Builds Without The Required Authorization.
* Do Not Exploit Town Or Nation Permissions To Gain Unauthorized Access To Protected Areas.
* Do Not Deliberately Obstruct Another Town's Legitimate Expansion Through Abusive Claiming Practices.
* Do Not Abuse Leadership Permissions To Deceive Residents Or Steal Their Belongings.
* Town Residents Should Respect The Rules And Reasonable Instructions Established By Their Town Leadership, Provided Those Instructions Do Not Violate Server Rules.

:::caution[Town Leadership Responsibility]
Mayors And Town Leadership Are Responsible For Managing Their Town's Permissions, Upkeep, Residents, And Infrastructure. Grant Permissions Only To Players You Trust. Staff May Investigate Reports Of Permission Abuse, Town Insiding, Or Intentional Damage.
:::

### Public Infrastructure & Shared Facilities

Public Infrastructure Helps Maintain An Accessible And Enjoyable Experience For Everyone.

This Includes Public Warps, Roads, Community Farms, Shared Grinding Areas, Transport Systems, And Other Facilities Intended For General Use.

The Following Activities Are Prohibited:

* Deliberately Destroying Or Damaging Public Infrastructure.
* Removing Blocks, Signs, Or Components From Public Facilities Without Authorization.
* Intentionally Blocking Public Entrances, Roads, Or Transport Routes.
* Modifying Shared Farms Or Machines In Ways That Prevent Other Players From Using Them.
* Claiming Public Facilities Or Their Surrounding Land Without Appropriate Permission.
* Using Public Facilities To Create Excessive Server Lag.
* Placing Inappropriate Or Offensive Structures In Public Areas.

:::note[Shared Facility Etiquette]
Players Should Leave Shared Facilities In Usable Condition After Using Them. Follow Any Instructions Displayed At Individual Facilities, Including Usage Limits And Maintenance Guidelines.
:::

### Events, Competitions & Rewards

Vista Valley May Host Events, Competitions, And Activities That Offer Rewards To Participating Players.

All Participants Are Expected To Compete Fairly.

The Following Activities Are Prohibited:

* Using Cheats Or Prohibited Modifications To Gain An Advantage During Events.
* Exploiting Event Mechanics To Obtain Rewards Multiple Times.
* Manipulating Competition Results Through Alternate Accounts.
* Deliberately Interfering With Another Participant's Progress.
* Providing False Information To Obtain Event Rewards.
* Colluding With Other Participants To Manipulate Rankings Or Outcomes.
* Abusing Event-Related Bugs Or Unintended Mechanics.

:::note[Event-Specific Rules]
Individual Events May Have Additional Rules, Restrictions, Or Eligibility Requirements. Instructions Provided By Event Organizers Must Be Followed Alongside The General Server Rules.
:::

### Bug Reporting & Exploit Disclosure

Players Play An Important Role In Maintaining The Stability And Fairness Of Vista Valley By Reporting Bugs And Unintended Mechanics.

When Discovering A Potential Vulnerability Or Exploit:

* Stop Using The Affected Mechanic Immediately.
* Record Relevant Information, Including Screenshots Or Videos Where Possible.
* Report The Issue Through A Ticket On Our [Discord Server](https://discord.vistavalley.net).
* Do Not Intentionally Reproduce The Exploit To Obtain Items, Currency, Or Other Benefits.
* Do Not Distribute Instructions That Enable Other Players To Abuse The Vulnerability.
* Cooperate With Staff If Additional Information Is Required.

:::danger[Exploiting Bugs]
Discovering A Bug Is Not Itself A Violation. Deliberately Abusing It, Concealing It, Or Sharing It For Exploitation After Discovery May Result In Severe Disciplinary Action.
:::

### Staff Interactions & Player Reports

The Staff Team Is Responsible For Maintaining A Fair And Safe Environment. Players Are Encouraged To Report Genuine Concerns Through The Appropriate Support Channels.

The Following Guidelines Apply:

* Provide Truthful And Relevant Information When Submitting Reports.
* Do Not Fabricate, Edit, Or Manipulate Evidence To Frame Another Player.
* Do Not Submit Repeated Reports To Harass Or Pressure Staff.
* Do Not Impersonate Staff Members Or Claim To Possess Permissions You Do Not Have.
* Do Not Interfere With Ongoing Investigations.
* Respect The Privacy Of Players Involved In Moderation Cases.
* Do Not Threaten Or Harass Staff Members Over Moderation Decisions.

:::note[Reporting Guidelines]
When Submitting A Report, Include The Player's Username, Approximate Date And Time, A Description Of The Incident, And Any Available Evidence. Clear And Relevant Evidence Helps Staff Investigate Reports More Efficiently.

Submit Reports Through Our [Discord Server](https://discord.vistavalley.net).
:::

### Server Performance & Infrastructure

Vista Valley Aims To Provide A Stable Gameplay Experience For All Players. Every Player Is Responsible For Ensuring That Their Activities Do Not Intentionally Disrupt Server Performance.

The Following Activities Are Prohibited:

* Intentionally Creating Machines Designed To Overload The Server.
* Deliberately Generating Excessive Entities To Cause Performance Issues.
* Exploiting Chunk-Loading Mechanics To Create Unnecessary Server Load.
* Building Malicious Redstone Contraptions Designed To Disrupt Server Operations.
* Repeatedly Triggering Resource-Intensive Mechanics To Cause Lag.
* Intentionally Attempting To Crash The Server, Individual Worlds, Or Specific Chunks.

Staff May Request That Players Modify Or Remove Structures That Cause Significant Performance Problems, Even If Those Structures Were Originally Built For Legitimate Gameplay.

:::warning[Performance Warnings]
If A Build, Farm, Or Machine Is Identified As Causing Significant Server Performance Issues, Staff May Request Modifications Or Temporary Shutdown. Players Are Expected To Cooperate Within The Timeframe Specified By Staff.
:::

### Punishments, Appeals & Rule Enforcement

Rule Violations May Result In Disciplinary Action Depending On Their Severity, Frequency, Intent, And Impact On The Community.

Possible Punishments Include:

* Verbal Warnings.
* Formal Warnings.
* Temporary Mutes.
* Temporary Suspensions Or Bans.
* Permanent Bans.
* Removal Of Illegally Obtained Items Or Currency.
* Rollbacks Of Affected Builds Or Structures.
* Removal Of Town Assets Or Other Server Privileges Where Appropriate.

Repeated Violations May Result In Increasingly Severe Punishments. Serious Violations, Including Cheating, Ban Evasion, Severe Harassment, And Intentional Exploitation, May Result In Immediate Permanent Bans.

:::caution[Appeals]
If You Believe A Punishment Was Issued Incorrectly, You May Submit An Appeal Through Our [Discord Server](https://discord.vistavalley.net). Include Your Minecraft Username, The Punishment Details, And Any Relevant Evidence.

Submitting An Appeal Does Not Guarantee That A Punishment Will Be Removed Or Reduced.
:::
