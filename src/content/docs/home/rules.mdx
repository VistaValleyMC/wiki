## Server Rules

If you have any questions about these rules, please ask a staff member on our [Discord server](https://discord.vistavalley.net) before taking any action that may violate them.

:::caution[Account Responsibility]
If you assist someone in breaking any of these rules or purposely ignore them, you will be punished in the same way they were. In the same vein, we will not accept someone else using your account as an appeal reason. Your account is your responsibility, and you must ensure it's secure at all times.
:::

:::caution[Group and Town Responsibility]
Vista Valley does not tolerate coordinated toxicity. If members of a town, nation, or alliance are found to be directly coordinating, encouraging, or repeatedly participating together in severe rule violations (such as hate speech, slurs, or targeted harassment), the entire group or town may face collective punishment. This includes permanent bans for all associated accounts and the complete deletion of town builds or assets. Choose your town members and associates carefully; their actions can sink your entire group.
:::

### Use Common Sense

This is the foundational rule. Even if something is not explicitly listed, it may still be enforceable. Staff have the final say, and severe violations may skip warning steps. If unsure about a rule or action, please ask. Honest mistakes are treated leniently, but ignorance is not an excuse.

### Chat Etiquette & Respectful Conduct

Good faith and light-hearted banter are welcome, but cease immediately if someone expresses discomfort, and apologize. This server is for relaxation and enjoyment; disrespectful behavior disrupts everyone's experience and fosters toxicity. Remember, there's a person on the other side of the screen.

Prohibited behavior includes, but is not limited to:

* Hate speech or language that attacks or demeans a person or group based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, disability, or other protected characteristics.
* Targeted insults, belittling language, or personal attacks intended to demean another user.
* Harassment, including repeated unwelcome contact, stalking, or creating a hostile environment.
* Language suggesting or implying physical harm or violence toward others.
* Suicide jokes or encouragement of self-destructive behavior.
* Language intended to demean or marginalize political or religious beliefs.
* Racial slurs and derogatory terms, regardless of context, jokes, or self-referential use.
* Glorification or excessive referencing of historical or contemporary ideologies that promote hatred, discrimination, or violence.
* Using languages other than English, Bahasa Melayu, or Bahasa Indonesia in public chat channels (other languages are permitted in private chat).
* Excessively pinging offline players for non-urgent matters, mentioning users who asked not to be pinged, or mass-pinging roles for general questions.

While swearing (basic profanity) is allowed in moderation, it must never cross into targeted harassment or the use of restricted terms. Under no circumstances are hate speech, racial slurs, or highly derogatory terms permitted anywhere on the server. This restriction is absolute, as it applies even if the words are used casually, in private, as a joke, or with the full consent of everyone involved in the conversation.

### No NSFW or Explicit Content

This Minecraft server is accessible to minors. Therefore, any content that is sexually suggestive, graphically violent, or otherwise intended for mature audiences is strictly prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to:

* Explicit textual descriptions.
* Sexualized images or videos.
* Graphic depictions of violence or gore.
* Links to external NSFW websites.

While private chat channels may have more relaxed conversations, any content that violates this rule and is reported will be treated as a global incident and subject to moderation. The safety and comfort of all users, especially minors, is our priority.

:::note[Private Messaging Policy]
Just like public chat, private messaging channels (such as local chat, town chat, and such) are subject to server rules. While staff may not be actively monitoring private logs at times, any hate speech, slurs, or targeted harassment reported via a support ticket will be treated as a global incident and moderated with standard server penalties.
:::

### No Hacking or Cheating

The use of any hacks, cheats, or unauthorized modifications that provide an unfair advantage over other players is strictly prohibited.

The server's anticheat may automatically kick players it flags as likely cheating, in addition to any staff review.

Please refer to the dropdown menus below for detailed information and specific examples.

<details>

<summary>Prohibited Modifications</summary>

The following modifications are strictly prohibited as they provide an unfair advantage or disrupt gameplay:

* Unverified modifications not explicitly approved by server staff.
* Bug exploits or glitches used to gain an unfair advantage.
* Nether roof exploits beyond what is explicitly permitted by server staff.
* Duplication glitches used to copy items outside intended in-game mechanics.
* PvP advantage modifications, including aimbots, killauras, speed hacks, and fly hacks.
* X-Ray modifications or resource packs that reveal hidden blocks and resources.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Allowed Modifications</summary>

The following modifications are permitted for use on the server:

* Minimaps and waypoint mods (surface terrain only, cave mode and entity tracking disabled).
* Recipe viewer mods such as JustEnoughItems (JEI) or NotEnoughItems (NEI).
* Performance optimization mods such as OptiFine or Sodium.
* Chunk caching mods such as Bobby.
* Fullbright mods for improved visibility in dark areas.
* Schematica or Litematica (hologram display only; printer/automated placement is prohibited).
* Cosmetic resource packs and shaders.
* Personal stat displays, inventory sorters, and shulker box peekers.

</details>

:::note[Additional Guidelines]
1. If you are unsure of the legality of a mod, resource pack, or shader pack, please ask before enabling it.
2. If a modification is not mentioned here, assume it is banned.
3. If you would like to propose an amendment to this list, please open a ticket on our [Discord server](https://discord.vistavalley.net). You will not be punished for asking.
:::

### No Griefing or Stealing in Protected Areas

Buildings in unclaimed land are subject to raiding (extracting items and breaking blocks to gain entry). However, raiding an unclaimed build does not exempt you from the [Earth Preservation Program](#earth-preservation-program). Destroying the surrounding landscape, lava casting, or flooding the area during a raid remains strictly prohibited.

Intentional destruction near town borders is prohibited and treated as griefing. Creating claims to deliberately hinder a town's expansion, such as encircling or walling off land to restrict their growth, is also forbidden.

:::note[Note on Shared Spaces]
Public grinding zones, shared warps, and open-world resource areas are accessible to all players. Competing for mob kills or loose experience orbs in these areas is considered regular gameplay and is not a moderatable offense. However, if a player repeatedly tracks, blocks, or follows you across multiple locations specifically to prevent you from playing, it may be reviewed under our Harassment policy.
:::

### No Unsolicited Advertising

We strictly prohibit unsolicited advertisements, including promoting external servers, websites, or services. Please refrain from any form of spam or self-promotion outside of designated channels or areas within our community. Responsible sharing of information within our community is welcome.

### Multiple Accounts, Cross-Platform Accounts, & Ban Evasion

Except for the single permitted Bedrock cross-play account outlined below, alternate accounts from the same IP address are strictly prohibited. Each account must have a unique owner. The use of bot accounts for any purpose is strictly forbidden.

Attempting to bypass or evade any active moderation penalty (such as bans, mutes, or temporary suspensions) using alternate accounts, VPNs, proxies, or shared accounts is strictly prohibited. Any alternate account used for ban evasion will be permanently banned immediately, and the penalty on your primary account will be significantly extended or upgraded to a permanent ban.

**Cross-Platform Accounts (Java and Bedrock):**

* Java Edition players are permitted to register one (1) alternate Bedrock Edition account for cross-play functionality.
* Information on joining the server via Bedrock Edition can be found [here](https://wiki.vistavalley.net/play-now#bedrock-edition).
* Your secondary Bedrock account must not be online while your primary Java account is active, except for limited circumstances such as town invites or pre-notified item swaps.
* Your secondary Bedrock account must reside within the same town (or nation) as your primary Java account.
* Your secondary Bedrock account cannot manage its own town.

### No Scamming

Scamming other players is strictly prohibited. All agreements must be upheld honestly and completely. This includes, but is not limited to:

* Deliberate deception or omission of details during trades.
* Selling renamed items to mislead others about their value.
* Selling mundane items as valuable or rare items (such as selling a candle as a "Capsule Key" for $5,000).

Obvious joke items, like "Holy Water" (a renamed bottle of water sold for $50), are permitted, provided they are clearly presented as a joke.

Valid proof of agreements is required, such as in-game chat screenshots or signed books. Discord screenshots can be used as supplementary evidence but are not considered primary proof due to verification limitations.

### No Sharing Personal Information

Sharing another person's personal information without their explicit consent is strictly prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to, addresses, passwords, and any other sensitive data.

### No Real-Money Trading (IRL Trading)

Trading in-game items, currency, towns, claims, or services for real-world money, gift cards, or external goods and services is strictly prohibited. The server economy relies on player effort and fair progression, and external financial deals disrupt this balance.

This includes, but is not limited to:

* Exchanging real-world currency, gift cards, or crypto for in-game currency, items, towns, or claims.
* Paying real money for building, mining, or in-game labor (or vice versa).
* Exchanging Vista Valley assets for items, currency, or accounts on other platforms or games.

Any attempts to facilitate, advertise, or execute real-money transactions will result in severe punishments, up to a permanent ban. The staff team will not investigate, mediate, or recover assets lost to external deals gone wrong. If you are approached by someone offering real money for your in-game assets, please report it via a ticket on our [Discord server](https://discord.vistavalley.net).

### Webstore Purchases & Chargeback Policy

All purchases made on the official Vista Valley webstore are voluntary and processed through PayNow. By purchasing virtual items, ranks, subscriptions, or perks, you agree that all transactions are final and non-refundable.

Guidelines regarding store purchases include:

* Subscriptions can be canceled at any time via the PayNow portal (`https://checkout.paynow.gg/subscriptions`). Cancellation stops future recurring payments but does not entitle you to a refund for past payments or prorated billing cycles.
* Initiating a chargeback, payment dispute, or unauthorized reversal through your bank, credit card issuer, or payment provider without prior approval from staff will result in an immediate, permanent ban across all associated accounts and IPs.
* If you encounter an issue with a transaction (such as missing items or checkout errors), please open a ticket on our [Discord server](https://discord.vistavalley.net) so staff can assist you before taking any external financial action.

### Respectful Combat

Player-versus-player (PvP) combat is prohibited unless explicitly agreed upon by all involved parties prior to engagement. Logging out during combat to evade conflict is also prohibited.

**Permitted Combat Scenarios:**

* Agreed-upon duels mutually consented to by all participants.
* Town outlaw combat initiated against players designated as outlaws within a town's jurisdiction.
* Declared wars occurring within the context of a formally declared war between towns or nations.

## Server Guidelines

### Earth Preservation Program

We have implemented many rules and systems to increase the longevity of the Earth map, ensuring we do not have to reset it while keeping it fun to play on.

To maintain the Earth's environment, we also limit certain activities above ground. We do not intend to reset the map, so ensuring it's well kept is pivotal for a positive experience for all. Activities that are prohibited include, but are not limited to:

* Creating forest fires.
* Flooding areas.
* Lava casting.
* Large-scale quarrying (within reason; this rule does not apply within your town's borders).

We also regulate some Towny aspects to keep Earth fair and habitable for all. This includes banning snake-claims and misuse of outposts.

* Snake claims are long, thin claims that usually connect two faraway claims or outline an area. Outlining with claims is allowed sometimes under the assumption it will be filled out within 14 days. Claims that are not substantially developed or filled in within 14 days of creation may be considered obstructive and subject to staff removal following a warning.
* Any land a town owns that is not connected to the main spawn must include an outpost plot. The exception is if there is a close-knit island chain or archipelago, where only one outpost plot or home block is needed. Contact staff if you are unsure about outpost legality.

#### Historic and Major Build Protection

The longevity of Vista Valley relies on preserving our community's history. Intentionally destroying, flattening, lava-casting, or looting long-standing towns, major historical landmarks, or highly detailed community builds—even if they have become temporarily unclaimed due to Towny upkeep failure or mayor inactivity—is strictly prohibited. 

If you discover a fallen town that clearly features significant historical or detailed structures, you must leave the builds intact. Players who deliberately obliterate server history will face permanent bans, and damage will be rolled back by staff. Open a ticket to ask staff before modifying any uncertain ruins.

### Redstone, Automation, & AFK Grinding

We maintain specific guidelines regarding automation and redstone machines to balance performance, quality of life, and fair gameplay.

:::caution[Automation & AFK Rules]
Automating player actions (such as attacking, placing blocks, or fishing) while away from your keyboard is strictly forbidden. This includes the use of auto-clickers, scripts, macros, or taping down a mouse button. However, passive AFK is permitted for activities that do not require automated input, such as standing in the [Glim Hub](/earth/gameplay/worlds/#glim-hub) for passive income or keeping chunks loaded for a redstone farm.

To reduce tedious grinding, you are permitted to use tools like an auto-clicker for specific tasks *only* while you are present at your computer and actively able to respond to staff. Permitted attended tasks include repetitive non-combat work (like breaking blocks in a generator or clearing terrain) and attacking mobs in a farm or grinder.
:::

<details>

<summary>Prohibited Machines and Farms</summary>

* Machines built with the intention of causing server lag.
* Machines made to bypass vanilla game mechanics, such as bedrock breakers.
* 0-tick farms or duplication mechanisms.
* Fast pulsing redstone clocks, including doubled observers acting as fast clocks.
* Farms utilizing excessive entity cramming.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Allowed Machines and Farms</summary>

* Semi-automatic farms requiring player interaction (e.g., standard cactus or sugarcane setups where players initiate planting).
* Fully automatic farms for crops that regrow without replanting (e.g., pumpkins, melons, bamboo).
* Mob grinders utilizing minimal redstone.
* Automatic smelting and item sorting systems.
* Non-redstone automated setups, such as Iron Golem farms and Villager breeders.
* Flying machines with a set endpoint, switch activation, and non-continuous movement.

</details>

:::danger[Laggy Towns]
If staff detect significant lag caused by a town, they will issue a warning to the owner(s), requesting adjustments within **24 hours**. Failure to comply may lead to staff intervention to preserve server performance. Proper management of farms, animal pens, redstone contraptions, and similar setups helps maintain a smoother experience for everyone.
:::
